Support

Here is how to do the most common things in Autism Nova. You open the parent area with the house button at the top right: press and hold until the ring is full. A short tap opens nothing, so it stays closed to your child.

Switching devices: taking your data with you

All data is stored only on the device. If you switch devices or delete the app, it is gone – unless you created a backup first.

  1. On the old device: open the parent area, scroll down to “Data” and tap “Create backup”.
  2. Save the file or send it to yourself, for example to the Files app or by email. The file contains your entries in plain text: only give it to people you would also show the log to.
  3. Install Autism Nova on the new device, open the parent area, choose “Restore backup” under “Data” and select the file.
  4. The app first shows what is in it (date, name, number of entries). Nothing is overwritten until you tap “Replace now”.

Every 30 days the app gently reminds you to make a new backup.

Changing the app's language

Autism Nova is available in English and in German. In the parent area there is a switch “Deutsch / English”. After switching, the app reloads. Your entries are kept. Learning progress in the ABC is saved separately for each language.

The read-aloud voice doesn't match the language or is missing

The voice comes from the device, not from the app. If nothing is read aloud or the voice does not match the chosen language (English or German):

Android

  1. Open Settings.
  2. “System” → “Languages & input” → “Text-to-speech output” (sometimes just “Speech”).
  3. Choose “Speech Services by Google” and install English or German under “Language”.
  4. Open Autism Nova again.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings.
  2. “Accessibility” → “Spoken Content” → “Voices”.
  3. Choose “English” or “German” and download a voice.
  4. Open Autism Nova again.

Windows

  1. Open Settings (Windows key + I) and search for “voices” at the top.
  2. Under “Manage voices”, click “Add voices” and choose an English or German voice.
  3. Close Autism Nova and start it again.

Withdrawing from your purchase

You can withdraw from your purchase within 14 days of the conclusion of the contract, insofar as you have a statutory right of withdrawal – without giving a reason and without signing in.

Withdraw from contract (page in German)

The same button is in the app in the parent area under “About Autism Nova”, together with an English translation of the withdrawal notice. On the website, the withdrawal notice, the model withdrawal form and the information on warranty and updates are on the page “Vertrag widerrufen” (in German). Please note: in the Apple App Store and on Google Play, you agree at checkout that the app is provided immediately; this can make the right of withdrawal expire early. The fastest way to get a refund is to ask the store directly.

Deleting everything

In the parent area under “Data” there is “Delete all data”. This removes everything on this device and cannot be undone. Create a backup first if you want to keep anything.

Something isn't working

Write to us what happened, on which page of the app and with which device (for example “Samsung tablet, Android 14”). The version number is in the parent area under “About the app”. Please do not send any information about your child and no backup file.

Email: information@autismnova.de

Important

Autism Nova is an everyday aid, not therapy and not a diagnosis. In case of acute danger, self-injury or an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in the EU, 911 in the USA). If you are worried about your child's development, your pediatrician or an early intervention service is the right place to go.
