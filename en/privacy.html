Privacy policy

As of: November 9, 2026

This is an English translation of our German privacy policy (Datenschutzerklärung). In case of doubt, the German version applies.

This policy has two parts: the Autism Nova app and this website. In short: the app does not collect any data about you, and this website uses no cookies and no tracking services.

2. The Autism Nova app

We do not collect, store or transmit any personal data through the app. Everything you enter in the app is stored exclusively in your device's storage. In detail:

  • All data stays on the device. Daily plan, log, diaries, learning progress and settings are stored only in the device's storage. There is no server that anything is uploaded to.
  • No accounts, no sign-in. You don't have to register or enter an email address.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics services. No advertising or statistics components are built in. We do not find out how often or how long the app is used.
  • Speech output by the operating system. Words and sentences that are read aloud are produced by your device's speech voice (Android, iOS or Windows). The text goes to the operating system, not to us. Whether and how your device processes speech output is governed by the settings and terms of the device manufacturer; offline voices can be chosen in the device settings.
  • Camera, photos, videos and microphone only at your request. The app only asks for the camera, photo library or microphone when you explicitly take or choose a photo, a short video or a voice recording of your own for a step. Everything stays on the device. Otherwise the camera and microphone stay off.
  • Notifications only if you switch them on. On Android and iOS, the app can show the “Three sayings a day” for parents as notifications. Your device schedules them itself – without a server and without a push service from Apple or Google; nothing is sent. The app only asks for permission to show notifications when you switch them on in the parent area under “For you”. On Android it may also restore scheduled notifications after a restart and briefly wake the device to do so.
  • The backup file only leaves the device if you share it. Under “Data” in the parent area you can save everything as one file. It contains your entries in plain text. It is stored wherever you save or send it – think about whom you give it to. The same applies to reports, the passport and the crisis plan, which you can print, copy or share.
  • No encryption on the device. The parent PIN protects against prying eyes, but it is not encryption. Whoever holds the unlocked device can read the data. A device passcode is therefore sensible.
  • Deleting: any time, completely. “Delete all data” in the parent area removes everything. If the app is uninstalled, the data is gone as well – so create a backup first.
  • “Internet” permission without any connection to us. On Android, “Internet” appears among the app's permissions. This permission belongs to the app's technical shell (Capacitor) and is needed so the app can load its own pages. The app does not connect to any server of ours – there is none.

Information about children

On request, Autism Nova stores information about your child (for example name, profile, behavior log, sleep and toilet diary). This is sensitive data, in part health data. It stays on the device and is evaluated by nobody. You alone decide whether and to whom you pass on a backup or a copied text. The app is aimed at parents and other adults who accompany a child; the parent area is protected by a button that has to be held down and, if you wish, by a PIN.

Purchase, crash reports and reviews through the stores

Apple, Google or Microsoft handle the purchase under their own privacy policies. We receive no details about individual buyers from them, only aggregated sales figures. On Apple and Android devices, we only receive crash and usage reports if you have allowed this in your device settings (Apple: “Share with App Developers”, Android: “Usage & diagnostics”). On Windows, Windows reports app crashes to Microsoft with its diagnostic data; we see counts and technical error details. The reports contain no name and no contact details. You write store reviews under your store profile; they are held by Apple, Google or Microsoft.

Withdrawing from the purchase

If you withdraw from the purchase (button “Withdraw from contract” on this website or in the app), your email program opens with a prepared text. Nothing is sent to a server of ours. Only when you send the email do we learn your name, your email address and the details of the purchase. We use them only to process the withdrawal and to confirm its receipt (Art. 6 (1) (b) and (c) GDPR) and keep them only as long as necessary for this and for statutory retention obligations. The withdrawal notice (in German) is on the page “Vertrag widerrufen”.

3. This website

When the site is accessed, GitHub receives information for technical reasons such as the IP address, date and time and the page requested; according to its own statements, GitHub stores the IP address for security purposes. This is necessary to deliver the site and to fend off abuse (Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR). We ourselves do not receive this data and do not evaluate anything. No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies, includes no statistics, advertising or social media services and loads no fonts or files from third-party servers. The font “Nunito” is hosted on our own server. On the page “Vertrag widerrufen” (withdraw from contract) a small script runs that only puts together the email text; it sends nothing to us.

Contact by email

If you write to us, we use your details only to answer your request (Art. 6 (1) (b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once the request is settled and no retention obligation applies. Please do not send any information about your child.

4. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, insofar as we process data of yours. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the authority responsible for us, the State Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information of North Rhine-Westphalia (Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen), or the authority where you live.

We make no automated decisions about you, pass nothing on to third parties and transfer no data to countries outside the EU. You are not obliged to give us any data; without details, the only thing we cannot do is answer a request.

5. Changes

If something about the app or this site changes that affects data protection, we will update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version.
