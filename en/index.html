What Autism Nova can do

The app has two sides: one for your child that works calmly and without pressure, and one for you as adults, behind a lock that a child won't open by accident.

For your child

  • Sounds for falling asleep, calming down and transitions.
  • Plan and supports: a visual daily schedule, first–then, a choice board, step by step, a “Just a little longer” timer with a gentle warning and a waiting card for the doctor, the hairdresser and the bus.
  • Feelings and body: a feelings traffic light, a calming plan and small exercises for sensing one's own body.
  • Talking: a speech board with core words in fixed places and a sentence bar. Your child does not have to speak – pointing and tapping are enough.
  • Learning and playing: numbers, tracing letters, drawing, movement games and picture books that can be read aloud.

For you as parents

  • Sleep program in nine modules, based on a tested parent program, with an evaluation of your sleep diary.
  • My passport: a profile you can print or share as a card for the doctor, school, the hairdresser or grandma.
  • Preparation packs for the dentist, hairdresser, hospital, starting school, travel and more – with a picture story and a checklist.
  • Crisis plan, log and reports: spot patterns without judging, and create a report for therapy or a doctor's appointment.
  • Playing together and modeling: short guides on how to use the speech board with your child.
  • For you: mindfulness minutes, a self-check and a guide to relief and support.
  • At a glance: your weeks, milestones and up to three goals.

The picture books

Ten short stories in gentle watercolor pictures – from falling asleep and riding the bus to the puddle. Each page has a short sentence, and below the sentence the words appear again as small picture cards. The page can be read aloud, or you read it yourself.

What it looks like

Calm colors, large areas, clear pictures. Nothing flashes, nothing runs out, no error sounds.

Our approach

Autism Nova is not meant to turn an autistic child into an inconspicuous one. It is meant to help your child show what they need – and to help you understand them.

Calm, not loud

No time pressure, no error sounds, no chasing points. If you have reduced motion on your device, you get a calmer app.

Honest, not promising

Each area says how well it is supported by evidence. We promise no cure and no progress.

You decide

You know your child. Everything can be switched on and off, repeated or ignored. Speaking is never a requirement.

How we label what we know

Well supportedSeveral studies or guidelines support it, for example visual schedules, warnings before transitions and video modeling.
PromisingFirst studies are positive, but small or not yet tested with autistic children.
Not claimedWhat is popular but not supported is labeled as such – for example “connecting the brain hemispheres”.

Autism Nova is not a medical device, not therapy and makes no diagnosis. The app does not replace medical, speech-therapy, occupational-therapy or psychological support.

Your data belongs to you

The app holds sensitive things: name, behavior, sleep, health. That is why everything stays on your device.

  • Everything stays on the device. There is no server that anything is uploaded to.
  • No account, no sign-in. You don't have to enter an email address.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics services. Nobody finds out how often you use the app – not even us.
  • Backups only when you want them. With one button you save everything as a file, for example for a new device.
  • Delete any time. One button in the parent area removes everything.

Frequently asked questions

What age is the app for?

The content is aimed at children from toddler age to primary school age. Autistic children are very different, so you decide which areas suit your child. Much of it also works for older children.

Does my child have to be able to speak?

No. All exercises can be answered by pointing and tapping. For children who speak little or not at all, there is the speech board.

Does the app need the internet?

No. Everything works without an internet connection.

What does it cost?

Autism Nova is bought once; there is no subscription and no advertising. In Germany it costs €24.99 in the App Store; the store converts the price for other countries. All updates are included.

Does the app replace therapy?

No. It is an everyday aid for families. If you are worried about your child's development, your pediatrician or an early intervention service is the right place to go.

Is the app available in English?

Yes. Autism Nova is available in English and in German. On a phone or tablet the app starts in the device's language; you can switch at any time in the parent area. Texts, picture books, the speech board, letters and numbers then change. The read-aloud voice comes from the device.

Can I withdraw from my purchase?

Yes, within 14 days of the conclusion of the contract, insofar as you have a statutory right of withdrawal. No reason, no sign-in: Withdraw from contract (page in German). The withdrawal notice and the model withdrawal form are there as well. Please note: in the Apple App Store and on Google Play, you agree at checkout that the app is provided immediately; this can make the right of withdrawal expire early.

What happens to my data when I switch devices?

It stays on the old device. First create a backup in the parent area under “Data” and restore it on the new device. Here is how, step by step.
